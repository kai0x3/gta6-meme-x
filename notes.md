# GTA 6 Meme — gta6-meme-x-01 (@kai0x3)

**Concept:** A Leonida beach at sunset: a fan who waited 13 years for GTA VI is now a skeleton on the sand, controller still in hand, in front of a beach TV flashing "GTA VI — OUT NOW". Beside him, huge and unbothered, stands the make.fun ghost — his AI agent — in sunglasses, controller in hand, playing day one. Captions: "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA VI" / "THE WAIT KILLED HIM. HE'S STILL PLAYING DAY ONE."

**Press-kit asset used:** the make.fun ghost mascot from `mark-1024.png`, downloaded from the official press kit (https://make.fun/press/make-fun-press-kit.zip, file `make-fun-press-kit/mark-1024.png`). It is the real artwork, composited unmodified except for extraction from its magenta disc (flood-fill + radial cut), with sunglasses and a controller drawn onto it so it acts as a character in the scene; "make.fun" is also written on the TV bezel. Verified recognisable at 480×480 via `preview-480.png` (ghost ≈ 220px wide there, coin + star + outline unmistakable, all captions legible).

**Originality / assets:** everything else was drawn programmatically by me with Python/PIL for this entry (sunset, sea, palms, TV, skeleton, captions in the OFL font Anton). "GTA VI" on the TV is my own lettering, not the game's logo. **No Rockstar/GTA screenshots, logos, artwork or characters were used.**
